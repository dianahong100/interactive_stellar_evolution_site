The supergiant's core has collapsed in on itself, and with temperatures rising to over 10 billion Kelvin, gamma rays shatter the nuclei of atoms, producing neutrons.

All this occurs faster than the blink of an eye.

s

These new neutrons resist further compression, and this sudden stop in compression causes an explosive outward shockwave.

Despite all this, the core remains and continues to evolve. The path it takes depends on its mass.
