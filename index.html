Out in the far ends of space, thousands, even millions, of light years away from where you exist right now lies a stellar nursery.

s

This nursery is known as a nebula.

s

It is made of cosmic dust and gas, the materials that form stars.

As time passes, the nebula cools and the outward force of pressure slowly whittles down, giving in to gravitational collapse. As it compresses, a new object is formed.
