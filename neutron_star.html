Neutron stars are the most dense object known in the universe, with the sole exception of black holes, of course.

s

That is because neutron stars form from lower mass remnant cores.

s

However, thanks to their great density, they have strong gravitational and magnetic fields.

A special type of neutron star is a pulsar. This is a neutron star that spins incredibly fast while emitting bright beams of light from their poles. Its spin, magnets, and winds power a pulsar wind nebula.
